| Flag | Default | Meaning |
|------|---------|---------|
| `--mode` | — | `smoke` \| `load` \| `soak` \| `failure` |
| `--step` | `all` | `low` \| `med` \| `high` \| `peak` \| `all` |
| `--concurrency` | (from step) | override concurrency |

| `--soak-minutes` | 10 | soak duration |

| `--tag` | timestamp | artifact label |
